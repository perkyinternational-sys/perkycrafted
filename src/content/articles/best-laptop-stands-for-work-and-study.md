A laptop stand can make a work or study desk feel more comfortable and organized. It raises the screen from the desk surface, creates additional space and can make it easier to arrange an external keyboard, mouse and other accessories.

The right choice depends on your available space, preferred screen height, laptop size and how frequently you move between locations.

## Why use a laptop stand?

Most laptops sit lower than a comfortable viewing position when placed directly on a desk. A stand can raise the screen and help create a more practical working arrangement.

Possible benefits include:

- A higher and more comfortable screen position
- Better use of limited desk space
- Improved airflow around the laptop
- A cleaner setup for an external keyboard and mouse
- Easier switching between sitting and standing arrangements
- Better organization for work, study and video calls

A stand alone does not create a complete ergonomic setup. Screen height, chair position, keyboard placement and regular movement also matter.

## What to look for in a laptop stand

### Stability

The stand should hold the laptop securely without excessive movement while typing or adjusting the screen.

Check the supported laptop size and weight before buying. Wider laptops may require a broader and heavier base.

### Height and viewing angle

A useful stand should raise the screen enough for your normal sitting position. Height-adjustable models offer more flexibility for different users and desks.

If the stand raises the keyboard significantly, an external keyboard and mouse will usually make the setup more practical.

### Adjustability

Some stands use fixed positions, while others allow changes to both height and angle.

More adjustment options can be helpful, but the hinges should remain firm enough to support the laptop without slowly lowering during use.

### Portability

Students, remote workers and travellers may prefer a folding stand that fits easily into a backpack.

For a permanent home-office desk, stability may be more important than low weight.

### Material and construction

Aluminium stands are commonly chosen for their strength, appearance and relatively low weight. Reinforced plastic stands may be lighter and more affordable.

Look for non-slip pads on the base and contact points to help protect the laptop and desk.

### Ventilation

The design should avoid blocking the laptop's ventilation openings. Open-frame stands generally provide better airflow than broad solid platforms.

## Main types of laptop stands

### Fixed stands

Fixed stands have a simple structure with little or no adjustment.

They can work well on a dedicated desk where the same person uses the laptop in the same position every day. Their simpler construction can also provide good stability.

### Height-adjustable stands

Adjustable stands allow the screen position to be changed for different desks, chairs or users.

They are suitable for permanent work and study setups, especially when used with an external keyboard and mouse.

### Portable folding stands

Folding stands are designed for carrying between home, college, libraries, offices and shared workspaces.

They are usually lighter and more compact, although very lightweight models may be less stable than heavier desk stands.

### Vertical laptop stands

A vertical stand stores a closed laptop upright. It is useful when the laptop is connected to an external monitor and does not need to remain open.

This type saves desk space but is not intended to raise an open laptop screen.

## Which type should you choose?

| Situation | Suitable option |
|---|---|
| Permanent home-office desk | Stable height-adjustable stand |
| Small study desk | Compact fixed or folding stand |
| Frequent travel or classes | Lightweight folding stand |
| Shared desk used by multiple people | Adjustable-height stand |
| Laptop connected to an external monitor | Vertical laptop stand |
| Occasional video calls | Simple adjustable stand |

## Practical buying checklist

Before purchasing, confirm:

- Your laptop's screen size and weight
- The stand's supported dimensions
- Whether height adjustment is required
- Whether you will use an external keyboard and mouse
- How much desk space is available
- Whether the stand needs to fit inside a bag
- Whether the base includes non-slip protection
- Whether ventilation openings remain unobstructed

## Final thoughts

Choose a laptop stand that supports your normal work or study routine without making the setup unnecessarily complicated.

A stable adjustable stand is generally more suitable for a permanent desk, while a compact folding model is more practical for students and people who frequently change locations. The best option is the one that securely supports your laptop and fits comfortably into the way you already work.
